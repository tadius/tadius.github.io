About the app

The MySalary app is made for people who want to know how much they really earn.

Just start using the app and add every payment on time — soon you will have a clear picture of your average annual income. Entering the information takes very little time.

The app lets you record income by category and income source.

You can edit them and add new ones.

All records can be filtered by the selected categories and income sources.

Planned and actual payments are supported.

The annual report gives you a complete summary of:

  • payment amounts for each month
  • quarterly income
  • average annual income

    • The annual report can be displayed by the selected categories and income sources.

    The app can create a local database backup.
